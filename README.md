<h2>📖 About YummWays</h2>

<p><strong>YummWays</strong> is a modern food-delivery platform website built around the idea of <strong>“Great Food. Fair Prices. Better Futures.”</strong> The website presents a transparent and interactive food-delivery experience for customers, restaurant owners, and delivery partners.</p>

<h2>✨ Features</h2> <ul> <li>🍔 Modern food-delivery landing page</li> <li>🎨 Clean and attractive UI</li> <li>✨ Animated buttons and interactive sections</li> <li>🍕 Food-themed floating images and visuals</li> <li>📱 Responsive design for desktop, tablet, and mobile</li> <li>📲 Google Play Store and Apple App Store buttons</li> <li>🔳 QR-code based app access</li> <li>🛵 Delivery-partner information</li> <li>🏪 Restaurant-owner information</li> <li>💰 Transparent pricing information</li> <li>🤝 Customer, restaurant, and delivery-partner focused sections</li> <li>🤖 Interactive Spline 3D robot in the contact section</li> <li>🌐 Social-media integration</li> </ul>

<h2>🧩 Website Sections</h2>

<h3>🏠 Hero / Landing Section</h3> <p>Introduces YummWays with the main message <strong>“Great food. Fair prices. Better futures.”</strong>, food visuals, animated elements, call-to-action buttons, and app-download options.</p>

<h3>🍔 Why YummWays</h3> <ul> <li>💳 Transparent Pricing</li> <li>🏪 Fairer Restaurant Economics</li> <li>🛵 Better Partner Pay</li> <li>🛠️ 24/7 Technical Support</li> </ul>

<h3>🔄 Platform Journey</h3> <p>Uses animated cards and visual elements to present different parts of the YummWays customer and partner journey.</p>

<h3>🤝 From Local Connections to Lasting Loyalty</h3> <p>Presents campus activations, social campaigns, referral rewards, and family-oriented offers through an animated card layout.</p>

<h3>💰 Pricing / Platform Comparison</h3> <p>Presents YummWays pricing and partner-economics information alongside comparison information about other food-delivery platforms.</p>

<h3>📲 App Download & QR Section</h3> <p>Provides access through Google Play, Apple App Store, and QR-code scanning.</p>

<h3>📞 Let's Connect</h3> <p>The contact section contains the YummWays location and mobile number together with an interactive Spline 3D robot that responds to cursor movement.</p>

<h3>🔗 Footer</h3> <p>The footer provides YummWays social-media links and contact information.</p>
